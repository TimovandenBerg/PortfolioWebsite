Circulera

Circulera with New Story

For a project at New Story I developed Circulera: a web-based knowledge platform designed to help small and medium-sized enterprises transition to a circular economy. Commissioned by Circulair West in collaboration with the provinces of North and South Holland, the project aimed to simplify access to reliable information on circular business practices.

Project Scope
The goal was to design and evaluate an intuitive user interface that serves as a smart knowledge base. It enables SMEs to ask questions and receive instant, relevant answers, while also storing previous Q&A for easy retrieval. This empowers businesses to navigate complex topics like resource management, sustainable product design, chain collaboration, and regulatory compliance.

My Role

  • Lead the UX research: conducted stakeholder interviews, user surveys, competitive analysis, journey mapping and affinity diagrams to map user needs. Facilitated co-creation sessions and iterated designs based on expert feedback.
  • Designed the full user interface: from wireframes and style guides to high-fidelity mockups.
  • Tested the Prototype with real users, applying usability heuristics and ISO 25010 standards, and iterated based on feedback.

    • Outcome
    By using Design Thinking this project demonstrated that the platform can enhance communication and knowledge acquisition for SMEs pursuing circular practices, provided that certain improvements continue to be implemented. This project gave me hands-on experience in UX research, UI design, and iterative development, working closely with business stakeholders, end users, and development teams to translate complex requirements into practical digital solutions.

    All the information has been set in a research report which I'm always glad to share with you.
