Circulera with New Story

During the internship I was responsible for improving the user experience in the application. It has been in development for over 6 years and someone had yet to research the user experience of it. I helped regain the vision on who the users are with interviews, after which I made user flows of the entire application to improve the efficiency of use. I also did an analyses with help of the 10 heuristics of J. Nielsen. For the final I used HotJar to get more insight in the overall usage and if the information that was extracted previously was coherent to these new insights.

After al these results I found a total of 79 problems inside the application that could dramatically improve the user experience. So for the remaining duration of the internship I went coding and designing and trying to resolve as many problems as possible.

All the information has been set in a research report which I’m always glad to share with you.
