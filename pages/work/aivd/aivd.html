AIVD

AIVD commission at A.L.S.R.V. Asopos de Vliet

For the redesign and the development of the mobile application for Asopos de Vliet I led the UX/UI team. A.L.S.R.V. Asopos de Vliet is a student association based in the Netherlands. This project involved collaborating closely with a team of fellow UX designers and developers, as well as engaging with various stakeholders from the association to ensure the app met their specific needs and enhanced the user experience for all members. The application was developed using Dart and Flutter, showcasing my dual expertise in design and front-end development.

My Role

  • Conducted analysis of existing user flows and identified pain points within the previous application.
  • Developed a modern, clean, and consistent visual design standard for the entire application, adhering to Asopos de Vliet's branding.
  • Created high-fidelity mockups and prototypes for all screens, ensuring a seamless and aesthetically pleasing interface.
  • Created a comprehensive style guide to ensure consistency across the application surrounding colors, typography and states for buttons and forms.
  • Collaborated with developers to integrate various functionalities and ensure cross-platform compatibility.
  • Presenting the design and development process.
