Timo van den Berg

I am a front-end developer and UX researcher based in Leiden, The Netherlands. I hold a BSc in Computer Science specialized in Interaction Technology, and I am currently broadening my perspective with a BA in Japanese Studies at Leiden University.

My strength lies in bridging human needs and technical reality. I combine thorough qualitative research and user journey mapping with hands-on front-end implementation to translate real pain points into intuitive, responsive interfaces. Whether designing component systems in Figma or building them out in React, Next.js, TypeScript, and modern CSS, I focus on solutions that genuinely help the person on the other side of the screen.

Years of national-level rowing and ice hockey taught me how to perform under pressure, stay disciplined, and rally a team toward a shared finish line. I bring that same drive to cross-functional product teams—collaborating smoothly with developers, designers, and stakeholders, even across cultures, languages, and complex domains.
